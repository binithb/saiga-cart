Drift · 2 / 9

Three Kinds of Drift

Without a shared, durable way of working, AI assistance pulls projects off course in three distinct ways. Each drift compounds as the system and team grow.

⚠️
The common root: All three drifts occur because the shared picture of the project lives nowhere durable — forcing every person and chat session to guess or reconstruct it.
Failure Modes

How Unanchored AI Creates Friction

01

People Drift Apart

Every developer prompts their AI differently. Without shared instructions, three developers in three chat sessions produce three conflicting architectures in one codebase.

02

AI Drifts from Intent

AI generates code faster than humans can deeply review. Without explicit boundaries and verification gates, rapid implementation magnifies unrecorded constraints and quickly diverges from agreed product intent.

03

Context Evaporates

Architectural decisions and trade-offs live only in chat scrollback. When the session closes, context vanishes — and the next session starts completely blind.
