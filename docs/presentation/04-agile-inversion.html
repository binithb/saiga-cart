The Balance · 5 / 11

AI Changes the Balance

Agile correctly rejected exhaustive documents that delayed learning and substituted ceremony for delivery. AI does not reverse that lesson. It changes which lightweight artifacts let humans direct and verify fast-generated implementation.

⚖
A selective rebalance: working software remains the goal, and feedback still changes the plan. Concise specifications and executable verification become more valuable when agents cannot share the team's unstated context.
A Changed Default, Not a New Dogma

What Becomes More Valuable

Agile value remains validAI-led development consequence
Working software over comprehensive documentation.Keep documentation short and current, but make the specification and testable Definition of Done explicit enough to govern generated code.
Individuals and interactions over process and tools.Humans still decide together; capture consequential constraints where every agent and future session can apply them.
Responding to change over following a plan.Adapt continuously, while recording material plan and decision changes so speed does not erase shared understanding.
Customer collaboration over contract negotiation.Continue customer collaboration; provide an AI implementation contract with interfaces, boundaries, safety expectations, and proof obligations.

A Specification Is a Shared Decision, Not Paperwork

In this guide, a specification is concise maintained context: what is true now, what changes next, why that choice was made, and how success is verified. It grows with risk and reach, not into a barrier to learning.

Source: The Agile-inversion lens is adapted from the Sputnik AI workspace presentation and Axel Molist's What 6 months of AI coding did to my dev team. This guide does not treat Agile Manifesto values as obsolete.
