The Shift · 4 / 9

The Bottleneck Moved

AI can generate implementation faster than a human can determine whether a large diff belongs in the system. The scarce work is making intent precise enough to direct, constrain, and prove the change.

↗
Oversight moves upstream: begin review with the design contract, then use the implementation and its evidence to confirm that contract was met.
What Changed

The Control Point Moves Upstream

Before Fast Generation

Delivery: Implementation time limits delivery.

Review: Much of the design is discovered from the diff.

Context: Code holds much of the team's implicit knowledge.

With Fast Generation

Delivery: Unambiguous intent and reliable verification limit safe delivery.

Review: Begin with the design contract; the diff demonstrates conformance.

Context: Record constraints so every human and agent session applies them consistently.

Rebalance the Work, Not the Goal

Customer discovery and feedback still lead; working software remains the goal. Keep specifications small and risk-scaled: outcome, boundaries, key decisions, and proof. A local edit may need only a clear task and focused test; migrations and cross-repository work need more. Update intent as you learn, rather than fixing every requirement up front.

Source: The bottleneck-shift and Agile-rebalancing framing is adapted from the Sputnik AI workspace presentation and Axel Molist's What 6 months of AI coding did to my dev team. This guide does not treat Agile Manifesto values as obsolete.
